About us

🌿

Experiences That Will Be Told As Stories
Over Dinner Tables For Years To Come.

Pamuzinda was born from a simple belief: that the wild places of Zimbabwe deserve to be shared and protected for generations to come. What began as a single lodge on the banks of the Manyame River has grown into a family of experiences spanning river, bush, and floodplain.

Today, our guests journey from the tranquil waters of Pamuzinda to the untamed wilderness of Mana Pools, from the comfort of Chengeta Safari Lodge to the gentle drift of our houseboats. Along the way, they become part of something bigger than a holiday.

Four Ways to Experience the Wild

Pamuzinda Safari Lodge

Our flagship retreat on the banks of the Saruwe River. Wake to the call of fish eagles.

Chengeta Safari Lodge

A secluded haven where comfort meets conservation. "Chengeta" means to preserve.

Mana Pools

A Zimbabwean Heritage Site. Walking safaris and elephants at the water's edge.

Our Houseboats

A different kind of safari. Drift along the water with the sounds of the rushing waves of Kariba.

Travel That Leaves a Mark

Through our partnership with a local community school, every guest stay contributes to something lasting: classrooms, learning materials, and opportunities. When you safari with us, you help shape Zimbabwe's future.
